Questions owners ask

How much does a fit-out cost?

Four things set the number. First, what the space already has: power, plumbing, venting and washrooms. Second, what your business needs that it doesn't: a restaurant needs a hood and grease interceptor, a daycare needs washrooms and finishes that pass licensing, a warehouse needs power and dock access. Third, permits. Fourth, finishes. On the call, we go through each one for your space, so you know what moves your number before you sign.

How long will my build take?

Permits and approvals are often the longest part. On the fit-out review, we map your timeline from permit to opening day, flag anything with a long lead time (equipment, sign approval, health authority sign-off), and tell you what can start while permits are in.

Should I call before I sign the lease?

Yes, that's the right time. Bring the listing. We'll tell you what to check in the space and what to ask the landlord about: how many rent-free weeks you get to build, whether there's a tenant improvement allowance, and what the landlord delivers versus what you build.

Is the fit-out review really free? What's the catch?

It's free and you're not committing to anything. You leave with a list of what your space needs, which permits apply and what to plan before opening. If you build with someone else, the list is still yours.

I have drawings. Can you just build it?

Yes. Send the drawings, and we'll go through them and price the build. If something your business needs is missing, we'll tell you before it turns into a change order.

What happens after I book?

We call within one business day to set a 30-minute time. If you have a listing or floor plan, have it ready.

Can you build while my current location stays open?

We sequence the work to keep disruption low. Tell us how you trade on the call, and we'll plan the build around it.
